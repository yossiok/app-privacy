Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

TextShot - Screenshot Text Search ("TextShot", "the app") is developed by YosSoft / LutraApps. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

On-Device Processing

TextShot performs all OCR (optical character recognition) entirely on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition. Your screenshots and photos are never uploaded to any server. The extracted text is stored in a local database on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No accounts or sign-ups are required
  • No names, email addresses, or personal identifiers are collected
  • No screenshot content or extracted text leaves your device
  • No usage analytics or telemetry are sent to our servers
  • No location data is collected

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • OCR text index: Extracted text from your screenshots, stored in a local Room/FTS4 database for search
  • App preferences: Settings such as selected OCR scripts, auto-detect toggle, and theme preference (stored via DataStore)
  • Pro purchase status: Whether the Pro upgrade has been purchased (verified through Google Play Billing)

You can clear all app data at any time through Android Settings > Apps > TextShot > Clear Data.

Android Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Access your photos and screenshots for OCR indexing
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE (dataSync): Run background OCR indexing with a visible notification
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Show progress and completion notifications for indexing

Advertising

The free version of TextShot displays a small banner ad provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location (derived from IP)
  • App usage data related to ad interactions

TextShot uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your consent before personalized advertising, in compliance with GDPR and other regulations. You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ad Settings or through your device's advertising settings.

The Pro upgrade removes all ads from the app.

In-App Purchases

TextShot offers a one-time Pro upgrade through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children's Privacy

TextShot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
